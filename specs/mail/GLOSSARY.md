# Glossary

> Not reviewed.

### Alias

Another address of a mailbox, whose mail arrives in that mailbox.

Avoid: [mailbox](#mailbox).

### Deadline

The date by which a mail asks for something, as its text states it.

### Mail

One message received in a mailbox, with its sender, its text and the files attached to it.

Avoid: [matter](#matter).

### Mailbox

An account where the user receives mail at an address of theirs, kept by a provider.

Avoid: [alias](#alias).

### Matter

One mail, or the mails that concern the same affair: the replies to one another, or the mails that carry the same order, parcel or file number.

Avoid: [subject](../dictation/GLOSSARY.md#subject), [mail](#mail).

### Notice

A matter whose mail asks nothing of the user, but states a fact that changes something for them.

Avoid: [request](#request).

### Planning

The application where the user plans their work and keeps their tasks.

### Provider

The company that keeps a mailbox and its trash.

### Put away

Said of a mail that asks nothing of the user and changes nothing for them: taken out of their sight, with its reason, without being deleted.

### Request

A matter whose mail asks something of the user.

Avoid: [notice](#notice), [task](#task).

### Settle

To end what a matter asks of the user, by taking it on as a task or by dropping it.

### Sort

To place each mail, without the user, as a request, a notice, or put away.

### Task

One thing to do, kept in the user's planning, on a day or with no day yet.

Avoid: [request](#request).

### Trash

The place of a mailbox, on its provider's side, where a mail waits until the provider deletes it after its own delay.

### Unverified sender

A sender whose mail fails the checks that its address is really theirs.
