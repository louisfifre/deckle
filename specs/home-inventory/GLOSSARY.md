# Glossary

> Not reviewed.

### Archive

To put away an item the user no longer has, so that it stops showing and stays in the inventory.

Avoid: [delete](#delete).

### Assistant

A program that reads what the user writes or says in their own words, and acts on it for them.

### Code

The short name of an item of a room, made of its room and its kind, which no other item shares and which never changes.

### Delete

To take out of the inventory an item that should not be in it.

Avoid: [archive](#archive).

### Inventory

The record of what a home holds: its rooms, the items in them, and how these items are linked.

### Item

Anything the inventory records.

### Room

A part of a home that the inventory names and that holds items.

### Shopping list

What the user needs to buy for their home and has not taken yet.

### Space

A place of a notes application, kept by the user, that holds their own records.

### Worksite

A piece of work on a home, from its estimate to its end.
