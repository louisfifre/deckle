# Tell what your home holds in your own words, and keep its whole inventory in your own space

> Not reviewed.

## Need

### Context of use

1. The [user](../GLOSSARY.md#user) looks after a home: its electrical network, its appliances, its plants, its [worksites](GLOSSARY.md#worksite) and its shopping. They tell what it holds by writing or dictating a free list, mistakes included, to an [assistant](GLOSSARY.md#assistant), never to the [product](../GLOSSARY.md#product) itself. Their [inventory](GLOSSARY.md#inventory) lives in their own [space](GLOSSARY.md#space). The product serves any home, not one.

### User need

1. The [user](../GLOSSARY.md#user) needs to tell what their home holds in their own words, in order to keep its whole [inventory](GLOSSARY.md#inventory), linked [item](GLOSSARY.md#item) to item, in their own [space](GLOSSARY.md#space).

### Intended outcome

1. They start the [inventory](GLOSSARY.md#inventory) of a home from nothing with one [gesture](../GLOSSARY.md#gesture), and tell it what they have straight away.
2. They describe a [room](GLOSSARY.md#room) as they see it: every [item](GLOSSARY.md#item) they named is in the inventory at once, and they get back what was written.
3. Nothing waits for their confirmation before it is written; a mistake is [deleted](GLOSSARY.md#delete) afterwards.
4. Each item of a room gets its [code](GLOSSARY.md#code) by itself.
5. An item is not recorded twice: a name too close to one already there is refused, unless they say it is another.
6. What the [product](../GLOSSARY.md#product) refuses, it refuses with what it would accept.
7. They give the name of a new appliance or plant, and what is known of it is filled in for them.
8. They say what they need to buy and what they took, and see their [shopping list](GLOSSARY.md#shopping-list).
9. A [worksite](GLOSSARY.md#worksite) ends with its real cost and its real effort, and they see the gap with the estimate.
10. What they no longer have is [archived](GLOSSARY.md#archive); only a mistake or an item taken off a wall is deleted.
11. Nothing of their inventory enters the product.

## Rules

1. [What the product shows is its real state.](../deckle.rules.md)
2. A [code](GLOSSARY.md#code), once given, never changes, and no two [items](GLOSSARY.md#item) share one.
3. Nothing of a description is written until all of it can be.
4. An item is [deleted](GLOSSARY.md#delete) only in two steps: the [product](../GLOSSARY.md#product) first shows what it would delete, then deletes it on the [user](../GLOSSARY.md#user)'s word.
5. An item that another item points to is not deleted while it is pointed to.
6. What changes the shape of the [space](GLOSSARY.md#space) itself is off unless the user turns it on, and each of its [actions](../GLOSSARY.md#action) waits for their word.
7. Nothing of the user's [inventory](GLOSSARY.md#inventory) is written anywhere but in their space.

## Workflow

### Starting an inventory

When it goes well:

1. On one [gesture](../GLOSSARY.md#gesture), the [product](../GLOSSARY.md#product) makes the [inventory](GLOSSARY.md#inventory) of their home in a [space](GLOSSARY.md#space) of theirs, ready to receive what they tell it.
2. The [user](../GLOSSARY.md#user) tells it what they have straight away, without opening the notes application.

### Describing a room

When it goes well:

1. The [user](../GLOSSARY.md#user) tells the [assistant](GLOSSARY.md#assistant) what a [room](GLOSSARY.md#room) holds, as they see it, mistakes included.
2. Every [item](GLOSSARY.md#item) they named is written at once in their [space](GLOSSARY.md#space), each item of the room with its [code](GLOSSARY.md#code). Nothing waits for their confirmation.
3. The [product](../GLOSSARY.md#product) gives back what it wrote.

Otherwise:

4. An item has a name too close to one already there: nothing of the description is written, and the product names the close items. The user says it is another, and it is written.
5. Something in the description cannot be recorded: nothing of it is written, and the product says what, and what it would accept.

### Recording something new

When it goes well:

1. The [user](../GLOSSARY.md#user) gives the name of a new appliance, or the name and photos of a plant.
2. What is known of it is filled in for them, and the [product](../GLOSSARY.md#product) gives back what it wrote.

### Shopping

When it goes well:

1. The [user](../GLOSSARY.md#user) says what they need to buy, and later what they took.
2. They ask for their [shopping list](GLOSSARY.md#shopping-list), and get what they need and have not taken yet.

### Ending a worksite

When it goes well:

1. The [user](../GLOSSARY.md#user) ends a [worksite](GLOSSARY.md#worksite) with its real cost and its real effort.
2. The [product](../GLOSSARY.md#product) gives back the gap between them and the estimate.

Otherwise:

3. The real cost or the real effort is missing: the worksite does not end, and the product says what is missing.

### Putting away and correcting

When it goes well:

1. What the [user](../GLOSSARY.md#user) no longer has is [archived](GLOSSARY.md#archive): it stops showing, and it stays in the [inventory](GLOSSARY.md#inventory).
2. An entry mistake, or an [item](GLOSSARY.md#item) taken off a wall, is [deleted](GLOSSARY.md#delete) in two steps: the [product](../GLOSSARY.md#product) shows what it would delete, then deletes it on their word.

Otherwise:

3. Another item points to the one they delete: it is not deleted, and the product names what points to it.

## How Deckle answers, a sample on two lines

### 1.3.2 They get back what was written

1. The [assistant](GLOSSARY.md#assistant) gives the [product](../GLOSSARY.md#product) the [items](GLOSSARY.md#item) it read in the [user](../GLOSSARY.md#user)'s description, the whole [room](GLOSSARY.md#room) at once.
2. The product checks every item before it writes the first one.
3. It then writes them all in the user's [space](GLOSSARY.md#space), and gives each item of the room the lowest [code](GLOSSARY.md#code) still free for its room and its kind.
4. It answers with every item it wrote and its code, and the assistant gives them back to the user.

### 1.3.5 Not recorded twice

1. Before it writes an [item](GLOSSARY.md#item), the [product](../GLOSSARY.md#product) compares its name with the names of the items of the same kind already in the [space](GLOSSARY.md#space), and with the other items of the same description.
2. Two names are too close when they read alike once case, accents and punctuation are set aside; the comparison leans toward refusing.
3. The items of a [room](GLOSSARY.md#room) are known by their [code](GLOSSARY.md#code), not by their name, so two of them may share a name.
4. When the [user](../GLOSSARY.md#user) says an item is another one, the product records it all the same.
