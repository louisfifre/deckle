# Glossary

### Action

What the product does once something starts it, a gesture of the user or the product itself. The gesture is the user's; the action it starts is the product's.

Avoid: [gesture](#gesture).

### Application

A program the user works in, such as an editor, a browser or a mail client.

Avoid: [product](#product), [system](#system).

### Gesture

What the user does with their body to start an action, such as pressing keys or touching the screen.

Avoid: [action](#action).

### Microphone

The device that picks up sound for the computer or the phone.

### Person

A human being, whether they interact with the product or not, such as someone who speaks near the user.

Avoid: [user](#user).

### Product

The whole of what is made and offered to the user: what they see, what they do with it and what it does for them.

Avoid: [application](#application), [system](#system).

### Rewrite

To change the words of a transcribed text in the way the user chose; a translation is a rewrite.

Avoid: [smooth](#smooth), [transcribe](#transcribe).

### Smooth

To clean a transcription automatically, before any rewrite, keeping the user's familiar language or not.

Avoid: [rewrite](#rewrite).

### System

The operating system of the user's computer or phone, with every application that runs on it.

Avoid: [application](#application).

### Transcribe

To turn speech into written text.

### User

A person who interacts with the product.

Avoid: [person](#person).
