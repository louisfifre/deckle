# Glossary

### Action

What the product does once something starts it, a gesture of the user or the product itself. The gesture is the user's; the action it starts is the product's. Avoid: gesture, for what the product does.

### Application

A program the user works in, such as an editor, a browser or a mail client. Avoid: system, for one program; product, for what is offered to the user.

### Gesture

What the user does with their body to start an action, such as pressing keys or touching the screen. The same gesture starts and stops a dictation; another marks a change of subject. Avoid: action, for what the user does.

### Microphone

The device that picks up sound for the computer or the phone.

### Person

A human being, whether they interact with the product or not, such as someone who speaks near the user. Avoid: user, for a person who does not interact with it.

### Product

The whole of what is made and offered to the user: what they see, what they do with it and what it does for them. Avoid: application and system, for the whole.

### Rewrite

To change the words of a transcribed text in the way the user chose; a translation is a rewrite. Avoid: transcribe, for a change of the words; smooth, for the cleaning the product does by itself.

### Smooth

To clean a transcription automatically, before any rewrite, keeping the user's familiar language or not. Avoid: rewrite, for this cleaning.

### System

The operating system of the user's computer or phone, with every application that runs on it. Avoid: application, for the whole.

### Transcribe

To turn speech into written text.

### User

A person who interacts with the product. Avoid: person, for the one who interacts with it.
