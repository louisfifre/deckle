# Glossary

### Change of subject

The moment the user moves from one subject to another, marked automatically or by one gesture of theirs. The text keeps the subjects apart.

### Dictation

What the user says between the gesture that starts it and the gesture that stops it, and the text it gives back.

### Hesitation

A short stop or a filler sound inside a sentence, while the user looks for their words. Avoid: pause, for a stop inside a sentence.

### Listening

The time during which the product takes in the user's voice, from the gesture that starts a dictation to the gesture that stops it. Avoid: dictation, for that time alone.

### Noise

Any sound that is not the user's voice, such as a rub on the microphone or a sound around them.

### Pause

A time when the user stops speaking, then speaks again. Avoid: silence, which says only that no voice is heard.

### Silence

A time when no voice is heard.

### Subject

What the user talks about, in a dictation.

### Utterance

A stretch of the user's voice between two silences, which the product transcribes in one piece. An utterance ends on a silence; the listening goes on.

### Voice

The sound of the user speaking, as the product hears it through the microphone. Avoid: noise.
