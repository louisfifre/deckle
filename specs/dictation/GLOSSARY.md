# Glossary

### Change of subject

The moment the user moves from one subject to another.

### Dictation

What the user says between the gesture that starts it and the gesture that stops it, and the text it gives back.

### Hesitation

A short stop or a filler sound inside a sentence, while the user looks for their words.

Avoid: [pause](#pause).

### Listening

The time during which the product takes in the user's voice, from the gesture that starts a dictation to the gesture that stops it.

Avoid: [dictation](#dictation).

### Noise

Any sound that is not the user's voice, such as a rub on the microphone or a sound around them.

### Pause

A time when the user stops speaking, then speaks again.

Avoid: [silence](#silence).

### Silence

A time when no voice is heard.

### Subject

What the user talks about, in a dictation.

### Utterance

A stretch of the user's voice between two silences.

### Voice

The sound of the user speaking, as the product hears it through the microphone.

Avoid: [noise](#noise).
