# Glossary

> Not reviewed.

### Edge

One of the four sides of the screen, top, bottom, left or right, with the band of the picture along it.

### Lamp

A light of the room whose colour and brightness the computer can set.

### Realistic

Said of a light that keeps to the colours of the screen, as a natural light would, from daylight to a small candle.

Avoid: [vivid](#vivid).

### Vivid

Said of a light whose colours are stronger than those of the screen, so that each colour of the picture shows in the room.

Avoid: [realistic](#realistic).
