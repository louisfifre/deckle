# Do the everyday jobs of your own computer from one entry point, with local tools you keep in hand

> Not reviewed.

## Need

### Context of use

1. The [user](GLOSSARY.md#user) works alone on their own computer. In a day they dictate text, deal with mail from several mailboxes, look after their folders and photos, and build several software [projects](projects/GLOSSARY.md#project). They are used to the [system](GLOSSARY.md#system) of their computer and to the [applications](GLOSSARY.md#application) they know on it, and expect the [product](GLOSSARY.md#product) to look and behave like them.

### User need

1. The [user](GLOSSARY.md#user) needs the jobs of their day on the computer gathered behind one entry point and running on it, in order to do them without paying for, or depending on, outside services.

### Intended outcome

1. Each job is reached from one entry point that is always at hand.
2. Every [window](GLOSSARY.md#window) of the [product](GLOSSARY.md#product) looks and behaves like a well-made [application](GLOSSARY.md#application) of their [system](GLOSSARY.md#system).
3. The product works on their computer: no part of it depends for good on an outside or paid service.
4. The product shows them only what they need: few things, and nothing to manage.
5. What the [user](GLOSSARY.md#user) keeps of their own leaves their computer only where a goal says so, and then never with what identifies them.
6. Nothing that cannot be undone happens without a [gesture](GLOSSARY.md#gesture) of theirs.
