# Do the everyday jobs of your own computer from one entry point, with local tools you keep in hand

## Need

Draft, not confirmed

**Context of use.** One person works alone on their own Windows machine. In a day they dictate text, deal with mail from several mailboxes, look after their folders and photos, and build several software projects. They are used to Windows 11 and PowerToys, and expect their tools to look and behave like them.

**User need.** The user needs the jobs of their day on the computer gathered behind one entry point and running on their own machine, in order to do them without paying for, or depending on, outside services.

**Intended outcome.**

- Each job is reached from one entry point that is always at hand.
- Each tool looks and behaves like a well-made Windows 11 app.
- The tools work on the machine: none depends for good on a cloud or paid service.
- Each tool shows only what the user needs: few things, and nothing to manage.
- What the user keeps of their own leaves the machine only where a goal says so, and then never with what identifies them.
- Nothing that cannot be undone happens without a gesture of theirs.

## Goals

- Speak as long as you want, over everything, and get back a well-framed text: `dictation/deckle-dictation.md`.
- Turn a text or recordings into the text you want, one at a time or many at once: `transcribe-and-rewrite/deckle-transcribe-and-rewrite.md`.
- See what your mail asks of you and settle it fast, with the rest put away by itself: `deckle-mail.md`.
- Know where you stand in your folders, and sort, clean and picture them in one familiar place: `folders.md`.
- Find any of your projects and reach its folders, its version and its scripts from one place: `deckle-projects.md`.
- Save what you copied as a file in a folder, with one paste: `paste-to-folder/deckle-paste-to-folder.md`.
- Ambient lighting, in progress: its goal is not written yet, and it has no spec.
- The inventory of a home, kept in Anytype: its goal is not written yet, and it has no spec.

What every window rests on:

- Move between the places of any Deckle window through a navigation you keep open or closed as you choose: `navigation-pane/deckle-navigation-pane.md`.
