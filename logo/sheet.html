Deckle — logo sheet

The mark has two forms, both bare, as Windows 11 icons are. Normal has four blades and stands everywhere: the taskbar, the tray and the title bars. Small has three, so its gaps stay open at 16 px; it is kept for reference and no icon uses it. A blade lights up while Deckle works: one while it records, two while it rewrites. The icon pixels carry the shadow from 48 px up.
