Deckle — logo sheet

The mark has two forms, both bare, as Windows 11 icons are. Normal has four blades and stands in the taskbar. Small has three, so its gaps stay open at 16 px, and stands in the tray and the title bars. A blade lights up while Deckle works: one while it records, two while it rewrites. The icon pixels carry the shadow from 48 px up.
