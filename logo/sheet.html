Deckle — logo sheet

The mark has one form, four blades, bare as Windows 11 icons are, and stands everywhere: the taskbar, the tray and the title bars. A blade lights up while Deckle works: one while it records, two while it rewrites. The icon pixels carry the shadow from 48 px up.
