# AGENTS.md — Deckle.Autocorrect

Vocabulary is normative in `CONTEXT.md` § Autocorrect. The observation posture and its why live in `JOURNAL.md`.

## Hard rules

- **Password surfaces are gated before buffering.** On a password control, keystrokes are neither decoded, buffered nor counted. This is a rule, not a setting.
- **No typed text on the default path.** Always-on events carry counts, lengths and reasons only. Words cross only on explicit opt-in, into a dedicated consented dataset, local, password-gated and purgeable. The only other persisted text is the personal dictionary, inspectable and removable.
- **Detailed operational logs never grant content consent.**

## Engine

- The literal always wins: a form valid in French, the English lexicon or the personal vocabulary is never touched.
- Ambiguity requires a margin, never a bare argmax.
- Blacklisted token classes (digits, single characters, identifier-cased and already-accented words) are never corrected.
- Correction runs in two stages, normative in `CONTEXT.md`.
- Undo goes through the correction inlay, never by intercepting Backspace. An undo writes a suppression so the correction never returns.

## Data

Lexicons under `Data/` are regenerated by the build-data gesture (`BuildDataMaintenanceTests`). Their licenses and sources are in the repository root `NOTICE.md`.
