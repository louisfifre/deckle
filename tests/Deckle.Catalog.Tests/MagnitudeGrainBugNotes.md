# Magnitude grain bug notes

## Magnitude values carried binary dust from the slider into the field, the model and settings.json

- **Trigger:** Moving the slider of a Setting.Magnitude control (seen on Ambient "Reaction speed", minimum 0.05, step 0.05, 2 decimals) to a detent whose position is not exact in binary.
- **Observed symptom:** The number field briefly showed a value with many zeros and digits at the end instead of a clean two-decimal figure, then later a clean one. The exact string was not captured and has not been reproduced; it is not established which of the paths below produced it. This is uncertain.
- **Cause:** Established by arithmetic, not by running the UI. `BuildMagnitude` copied the slider's raw `double` (`e.NewValue`) into the NumberBox and into the model setter. Positions of the form `minimum + k * step` are not exact: on the Reaction speed grain 9 of 20 positions differ from `Math.Round(value, 2)`, for example k=2 gives `0.15000000000000002` and k=11 gives `0.6000000000000001` ("R"). Exposure (-2..2, step 0.1) and a 0..30 integer range show none with this formula; stepping by repeated addition gives dust on 12 of 20 Reaction speed positions and 39 of 41 Exposure positions. Which formula the WinUI Slider uses is not verified. The field's `DecimalFormatter` had `FractionDigits` set but no `NumberRounder`, and `FractionDigits` is documented as a minimum number of digits, so the formatter does not cap the display. Microsoft's NumberBox guidance pairs the formatter with an `IncrementNumberRounder`. The dusty value was also persisted and compared exactly (`PresetFamily.Match`, `RecomputeCanReset`).
- **Violated invariant:** A Magnitude value always sits on its display grain: the value stored, compared and persisted is the one the field shows.
- **Recurrence cue:** A numeric control that forwards a raw slider or NumberBox `double` to a setter, or a `DecimalFormatter` with `FractionDigits` and no rounder. Also a value typed finer than the grain, which displays rounded while the model keeps the longer figure.
- **Regression coverage:** `MagnitudeGrainTests.SliderPositionOnTheGrainStoresTheExactDecimalValue` snaps every slider position of the Reaction speed, Exposure and 0..30 grains and asserts the exact decimal value. The display rounder and the wiring in `SettingsComposer.BuildMagnitude` are not covered by an automated test; a settings.json written before the fix keeps its dust until the next edit.
