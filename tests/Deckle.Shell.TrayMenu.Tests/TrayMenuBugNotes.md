# Tray menu bug notes

## Quick-actions panel shows nothing on left click

- **Trigger:** A left click on the tray icon, from v0.39.0 (the panel's first release, built and merged without a run on screen) to v0.42.0. Louis, 2026-10-12: "au clic gauche, il se passe rien"; 2026-10-13, the same.
- **Observed symptom:** No panel appears, on every click. The journals hold no `Deckle-TrayMenu` event for the panel (Verbose, gated by the Windowing activity, off) and neither of the two non-gated warnings added on 2026-10-12 (`PanelDismissedWhileOpening`, `PanelSizeRejected`), while the menu's `PresenterSizeRejected` warning does reach them: nothing failed. A read-only enumeration of the running v0.42.0's windows (2026-10-13) showed the panel's carrier sized (190 x 368 DIP) and placed tangent to the icon by a `Show()`, so the click did reach the host, and showed the menu's carrier owning a `PopupWindowSiteBridge` window ("Pop-upHost") where the panel's carrier owned none.
- **Cause:** Established. `FlyoutBase.ShouldConstrainToRootBounds` defaults to `true` for a `Flyout` and to `false` for a `MenuFlyout` (Microsoft Learn). A constrained flyout draws inside its XAML root's HWND; an unconstrained one gets a top-level popup window of its own. The panel's `Flyout` therefore drew inside the carrier, a WS_EX_LAYERED window at alpha 0, invisible by construction, while the menu's `MenuFlyout` drew in its own popup window. `Show()` completed and the flyout was open; from the code's point of view nothing was wrong. The two 2026-10-12 hypotheses (focus moved into the popup, degenerate presenter capture) were not the cause; their guards stay, harmless. Fixed by `ShouldConstrainToRootBounds = false` on the panel's flyout, set before its first show.
- **Violated invariant:** A click on the tray icon opens its surface, and the surface closes only on a dismiss the user caused.
- **Recurrence cue:** a `Flyout` (not a `MenuFlyout`) shown on a carrier whose visibility relies on a popup window of its own, without `ShouldConstrainToRootBounds = false` before its first show; a session whose journal shows no line at all for a silent open (nothing fails when a surface draws where nobody sees it).
- **Regression coverage:** none through a natural seam: the property is set on a WinUI `Flyout` in `TrayQuickActionsHost`'s constructor and needs a XAML window. The manual check is the panel appearing on a left click; a read-only listing of the process's windows then shows a `PopupWindowSiteBridge` owned by the panel's carrier, as the menu's has.

## Context menu opens one item tall and scrolls

- **Trigger:** The first right click after launch, on every launch (v0.40.0 to v0.42.0, journals of 2026-10-12 and 2026-10-13); a click elsewhere and a second right click open it right. Over the months before, Louis saw it a few times, then on every open for a while, then once and briefly.
- **Observed symptom:** The menu card is one item tall (about 32 DIP) and its items scroll inside it. Every session since the evening of 2026-10-12 holds exactly one `PresenterSizeRejected` warning, shortly after launch (00:08, 00:17, 11:24, 18:06, 18:24 on 2026-10-13): at the first open the `MenuFlyoutPresenter` itself measured smaller than its items, whatever the carrier's size. The one recorded session with the Windowing activity on (2026-07-16, `app.jsonl.0001`) showed good opens, items 32 DIP and separators 3 DIP, presenter 179.5 x 268 DIP, with the prime cycle about 150 ms after construction, before any open.
- **Cause:** Established in the WinUI source for the mechanism; the island's value at that instant is read by inference, not observed. Under `FlyoutPlacementMode.Full`, `FlyoutBase::Open` sets the presenter's `Width` and `Height` to 1, then `PerformPlacement` replaces them with the rect `CalculateAvailableWindowRect` reads from `CXamlIslandRoot::GetSize()`, the XAML island's live size at the moment of `ShowAt`, never re-read when the window is resized afterwards (`dxaml/xcp/dxaml/lib/FlyoutBase_partial.cpp`, microsoft-ui-xaml, main). `MenuFlyoutPresenter` has `MinHeight = MenuFlyoutThemeMinHeight` (32 DIP), and in XAML layout a Min beats a smaller Width or Max. The carrier is resized by `MoveAndResize` right before `ShowAt`; on the first open after launch its island, never shown before, does not yet report that size (zero or stale), so the presenter is forced below its items and falls to one item; the second open finds the island at the size of the first and is right. The prime cycle opens with the default placement, not Full, so its capture is sound, which is why no `prime` rejection ever appears. Before 2026-10-12 the carrier also kept a first degenerate capture for the process lifetime (the `> 0` check), which explains "then on every open". Fixed by opening the flyout only once `XamlRoot` reports the requested size (`TrayCarrierWindow`, bounded wait).
- **Violated invariant:** The presenter is never sized below the items it shows; the carrier window is never sized below them either.
- **Recurrence cue:** a flyout shown with Placement Full right after its target window was resized or shown for the first time, without waiting for `XamlRoot` to report the new size; any one-shot measurement cached for the process lifetime without a check against what it must hold; a positivity check standing in for a size check on a control with a MinHeight; a `CarrierOpenedUnsized` warning in the journal (the island did not answer within the grace period, the card may have shown one item).
- **Regression coverage:** `TrayIslandSizingTests.AnIslandStillAtZeroDoesNotHoldTheRequest` drives `TrayIslandSizing.MatchesRequest`, the rule `TrayCarrierWindow` applies before opening the flyout; `TrayMenuSizingTests.CapturedSizeSmallerThanItsItemsIsNeverUsed` drives the carrier size decision. Whether the island reports its size within the grace period stays a manual check: with the Windowing activity on, `carrier open deferred` then `carrier island sized` frame the first open.
