# Tray menu bug notes

## Quick-actions panel shows nothing on left click

- **Trigger:** A left click on the tray icon, from v0.39.0 (the panel's first release, built and merged without a run on screen) to v0.42.0. Louis, 2026-10-12: "au clic gauche, il se passe rien"; 2026-10-13, the same.
- **Observed symptom:** No panel appears, on every click. The journals hold no `Deckle-TrayMenu` event for the panel (Verbose, gated by the Windowing activity, off) and neither of the two non-gated warnings added on 2026-10-12, while the menu's own warning does reach them: nothing failed. A read-only enumeration of the running v0.42.0's windows (2026-10-13) showed the panel's carrier sized (190 x 368 DIP) and placed tangent to the icon by a `Show()`, so the click did reach the host, and showed the menu's carrier owning a `PopupWindowSiteBridge` window where the panel's carrier owned none.
- **Cause:** Established. `FlyoutBase.ShouldConstrainToRootBounds` defaults to `true` for a `Flyout` and to `false` for a `MenuFlyout` (Microsoft Learn). A constrained flyout draws inside its XAML root's HWND; an unconstrained one gets a top-level popup window of its own. The panel's `Flyout` therefore drew inside the carrier, a WS_EX_LAYERED window at alpha 0, invisible by construction, while the menu's `MenuFlyout` drew in its own popup window. `Show()` completed and the flyout was open; from the code's point of view nothing was wrong. Setting the property to `false` (v0.46.1) made the panel visible on v0.48.0, which confirmed the cause on screen.
- **Outcome (2026-10-13, evening):** the visible panel was not what Louis wanted (full-size ToggleSwitch rows, an accent Record button: "beaucoup trop gros"; he wants list items, grouped, "même framework" as the right-click menu, inventory TR-01 and TR-03). Both surfaces are now instances of `TrayMenuHost`, a native `MenuFlyout` at its natural size; the panel, its `Flyout` and the sized carrier are gone. Seen on screen 2026-10-14: the items are right, but the `ToggleMenuFlyoutItem` check mark was rejected (its reserved column leaves a gap left of every command, and a switch cannot be told from a command) and the centered placement read as "opened at the click". The switches are `TraySwitchMenuItem` rows (the pill of 2026-05, now a templated control) and the menu opens edge-aligned, its corner on the icon.
- **Violated invariant:** A click on the tray icon opens its surface, and the surface closes only on a dismiss the user caused.
- **Recurrence cue:** a `Flyout` (not a `MenuFlyout`) shown from the invisible anchor window, which only an unconstrained flyout may use; a session whose journal shows no line at all for a silent open (nothing fails when a surface draws where nobody sees it).
- **Regression coverage:** none through a natural seam: the surface is now a `MenuFlyout`, unconstrained by default, built in `TrayMenuHost`, which needs a XAML window. The manual check is the menu appearing on a left click; a read-only listing of the process's windows then shows a `PopupWindowSiteBridge` owned by each anchor window.

## Context menu opens one item tall and scrolls

- **Trigger:** The first right click after launch, on every launch (v0.40.0 to v0.48.0, journals of 2026-10-12 and 2026-10-13); a click elsewhere and a second right click open it right. Over the months before, Louis saw it a few times, then on every open for a while, then once and briefly.
- **Observed symptom:** The menu card is one item tall (about 32 DIP) and its items scroll inside it. Every session from the evening of 2026-10-12 to v0.48.0 holds exactly one `PresenterSizeRejected` warning, shortly after launch: at the first open the `MenuFlyoutPresenter` itself measured smaller than its items, whatever the carrier's size. The one recorded session with the Windowing activity on (2026-07-16, `app.jsonl.0001`) showed good opens, items 32 DIP and separators 3 DIP, presenter 179.5 x 268 DIP, with the prime cycle about 150 ms after construction, before any open.
- **Cause:** Located, not pinned to one event. The menu was shown with `FlyoutPlacementMode.Full` on a carrier window sized by hand to the menu, so the visible card was whatever size WinUI gave the presenter under Full: `FlyoutBase::Open` sets the presenter's `Width` and `Height` to 1, then `PerformPlacement` replaces them with the rect `CalculateAvailableWindowRect` reads from the XAML island (`dxaml/xcp/dxaml/lib/FlyoutBase_partial.cpp`, microsoft-ui-xaml, main); `MenuFlyoutPresenter` has `MinHeight = MenuFlyoutThemeMinHeight` (32 DIP), and in XAML layout a Min beats a smaller Width or Max. Which value that rect took at the first open after launch is not observed: the 2026-10-13 hypothesis "the island does not yet report the size `MoveAndResize` has just set" was shipped as a wait on `XamlRoot.Changed` (v0.46.1) and did not hold (v0.48.0: the warning again at the first open, and no deferral trace, so the island already reported the requested size). Before 2026-10-12 the carrier also kept a first degenerate capture for the process lifetime (the `> 0` check), which explains "then on every open".
- **Resolution (2026-10-13, evening):** the mechanism is removed rather than patched a fourth time. Both menus are plain `MenuFlyout`s at their natural size, opened from an invisible anchor window placed on the tray icon's rect, on the side facing the work area (`TrayMenuPlacement`); WinUI measures and places them as it does for any menu on a button. No Placement Full, no prime cycle, no presenter capture, no carrier sizing, no island wait. Not yet seen on screen.
- **Violated invariant:** The presenter is never sized below the items it shows.
- **Recurrence cue:** any return to sizing a flyout from a window of ours (Placement Full on a carrier sized by hand, a captured presenter size, a cached measurement); a one-shot measurement kept for the process lifetime; a positivity check standing in for a size check on a control with a MinHeight.
- **Regression coverage:** none through a natural seam: the size now comes from WinUI's own menu layout. `TrayMenuPlacementTests` covers the side the menu opens on for each taskbar edge, the one decision this module still takes about where the menu goes. The manual check is the first right click after launch showing the whole menu, on a taskbar at the bottom and on one on the left.
