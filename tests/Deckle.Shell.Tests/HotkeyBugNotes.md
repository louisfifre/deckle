# Hotkey bug notes

## A hotkey pressed over the screen saver did nothing visible

- **Trigger:** A dictation runs longer than the screen saver timeout (60 s on Louis's machine, Helios.scr, not secure). The screen saver starts over the recording, and the Record hotkey (backtick alone) is pressed to stop it.
- **Observed symptom:** Louis reported on 2026-11-20 that the press did not stop the dictation and that a backtick was typed into the text field instead. The log of that day does not show the moment: no hotkey press is missing from it, so the exact sequence he saw is not reconstructed.
- **Cause:** Measured with probes on 2026-11-20 (Windows 11 24H2). Windows runs the screen saver on a desktop of its own, `Screen-saver`, the non-secure one included; Microsoft Learn says non-secure screen savers stay on `Default`, and the measure contradicts it. A hotkey consumes its key, so the screen saver never sees the press and keeps showing; only a thread of the screen saver's desktop may move the mouse to close it (SendInput from `Default`: ERROR_ACCESS_DENIED). An injected press over the screen saver did fire a chord held on `Default`; whether a physical press does is not measured, so how the backtick reached the field is inferred, not observed.
- **Violated invariant:** A hotkey works wherever the person can press it, and a press over the screen saver also ends it, like any other key. See the comments on `HotkeyDesktopPolicy` and `ScreenSaverHotkeyRelay`.
- **Recurrence cue:** A recording that "did not stop" while the screen saver showed; a log with `InputDesktopSwitched desktop=Screen-saver` and no `ScreenSaverHotkeysFollowed` after it; `ScreenSaverHotkeysFailed` or `ScreenSaverDismissFailed` warnings; a probe reading `Screen-saver` as the input desktop.
- **Regression coverage:** `HotkeyDesktopPolicyTests` pins which desktops the hotkeys follow. The move to the screen saver's desktop, the press and the closing need the real screen saver and an injected key from its desktop; they were checked end to end with a harness outside the suite (the screen saver started through `SC_SCREENSAVE`, F23 injected from its desktop: action received, screen saver closed within 150 ms, cursor back in place) and have no test in the suite, since a test would black the screen of the machine running it.
