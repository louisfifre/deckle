# Utterance countdown bug notes

## A dark band swept through the gauge while its cone spun

- **Trigger:** a pause in dictation long enough to open the gauge, with the default material (OKLCh L 0.75, C 0.3) graded at Saturation 0.
- **Observed symptom:** reported by the maintainer (2026-10-13): a darker zone, "the red, I think", crossed the lit wedges slowly and read as a hole in the outline. Not reproduced by an agent; nothing was run on screen.
- **Cause (computed from the code, not observed):** the countdown spun its cone with the Transcribing hue rotation (14 s) while the Recording outline it replaces keeps its cone frozen. The cone is baked from OKLCh at a chroma no sRGB hue carries at that lightness; `ColorSpace.OklchToRgb` clamps each channel, so the clamped colours no longer share one luminance. Desaturated with Rec. 709 weights, the wheel runs from a grey near 0.24 (hue 0.04, the red band) to near 0.69 (hue 0.54, cyan). The ordering holds for any luma formula: the red band has almost no green. At phase 0 the red band sits at 3 o'clock, inside the outline's fade-out; the rotation carried it through 12 and 6 o'clock, where the wedges are brightest.
- **Invariant:** the gauge is the Recording outline opened by the pause: same cone, same pose, same fade, same grading, read from one mapping.
- **Fix:** `CreateUtteranceCountdownVisual` paints and poses its cone through `BuildRecordingPaintConfig`, the mapping `CreateRecordingStroke` uses: frozen at `HuePhaseTurns` unless `RecordingHuePeriodSeconds` is set, graded as Recording, wedges fading in the outline's proportion (`GetUtteranceCountdownMaskConfig`). The Playground's Countdown material view shows the same frozen cone.
- **Root fix (same day, on the maintainer's decision):** the palette is baked with `ColorSpace.OklchToRgbInGamut`, which lowers the chroma per hue until the colour fits sRGB and keeps the lightness. The desaturated wheel is one flat grey, so the band no longer exists at any rotation; the chroma knob became a ceiling. Going back to RGB, the maintainer's first idea, would have widened the spread: a full-saturation HSV wheel desaturates to greys from 0.07 (blue) to 0.93 (yellow).
- **Superseded (2026-10-14, on the maintainer's decision):** the grey variants no longer desaturate the wheel at all. They show a flat grey material (`HudComposition.GreyMaterialColor`, one lightness per theme) mixed with the wheel by a `CrossFadeEffect`, so no lightness spread of the wheel can reach them. The wheel went back to the clamping conversion, because the gamut mapping bleached the red to pink in colour, which the maintainer read as a hole in the wheel. The band cannot come back through the palette: the grey is not computed from it.
- **Recurrence cue:** a grey variant that derives its grey from the colour wheel again (a Saturation or Grayscale effect on the cone); a visual that samples the cone through a rotation the Recording outline does not have.
- **Regression coverage:** `ConicPaletteTests.TheGreyMaterialIsOneGreyAtAnyLightness` pins that the grey material is a neutral colour, white at lightness 1 and black at 0, computed without the wheel. `UtteranceCountdownGeometryTests.MaskFadesKeepTheRecordingOutlinesProportion` pins the fade mapping. The pose and the grading are Composition objects with no headless seam. Manual check: Playground ▸ HUD ▸ Countdown material shows a still, evenly grey cone; Utterance countdown at 100 % shows no dark band.
